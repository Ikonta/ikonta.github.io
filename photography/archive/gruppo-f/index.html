F12 / Gruppo F

An exhibition, an international photographic conversation and a place to publish work that deserved a closer look.

From F12 to Gruppo F

I took part in the F12 exhibition at Galleri Korn, Stockholm, in 2007. That photographic network became part of the background to Gruppo F and The F Blog, which I founded and edited from the late 2000s onward.

The blog brought together photographers in several countries and offered a space for selected photographs, portfolios and conversations with other photographers. Quality of seeing and the individual voice mattered more than stylistic uniformity.

An editorial commitment

Gruppo F was not intended as a comprehensive survey of contemporary photography. It was a curated selection: work we wanted to spend time with, and to bring to other photographers' attention.

One of the longer features was my 2008 interview with Gunnar Smoliansky, published in January 2009. Earlier collaborative work, including photographs made with Joakim Sebring, was also revisited on the blog.
