F12 / Gruppo F

A photographic collective and an editorial platform.

I participated in the F12 exhibition at Galleri Korn, Stockholm, in 2007. The network later contributed to Gruppo F, an international photographic collective and The F Blog.

Gruppo F presented selected photographs, portfolios and interviews. It was intended as a curated conversation rather than a comprehensive survey.

Among the published work was my conversation with Gunnar Smoliansky, released in January 2009.
