Reminiscences of the Silent Landscape

Hågkomster av det tysta landskapet · photographs and texts with Joakim Sebring.

Two photographic voices

The collaboration with Joakim Sebring began in 2004, before Gruppo F, through discussions and the exchange of physical photographic prints. My black-and-white photographs met his colour photographs and texts.

We developed a sequence in which memories of places, childhood and journeys interacted with documentary fragments. The shifts between colour and monochrome became part of the work's changing sense of presence and distance.

Sequence rather than illustration

The images were arranged to create meaning through their relationships, without turning one photographer's work into an illustration of the other's. Text, recollection and documentary material introduced further tensions into the sequence.

The work was revisited on Gruppo F in January 2009 with Joakim's agreement. This archive note records the collaboration without reproducing the original sequence.
