Reminiscences of the Silent Landscape

Hågkomster av det tysta landskapet · with Joakim Sebring.

The collaboration began in 2004 with the exchange of physical prints. My black-and-white photographs and Joakim Sebring’s colour images developed into a sequence with accompanying texts.

We approached memories of places, childhood and journeys as relationships between images, rather than as illustrations of a fixed story.

The work was revisited on Gruppo F in January 2009 with Joakim’s agreement.

The original collaborative image sequence is not reproduced on this site.
