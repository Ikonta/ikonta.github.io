Objects for thinking

“Some questions become easier to ask when the answer has a shape.”

Three-dimensional models and tangible learning materials developed for activities in Japan.

Project

Physical models can connect mathematical and scientific abstractions with manipulation and group discussion. This collection is reserved for the Japan-related objects and STL files.

Individual files will be included only after their designs, authorship and revisions have been verified. This avoids presenting conceptual graphics as finished models.
