Mentor Reflex

“Repair begins by taking an unfamiliar mechanism seriously.”

Mechanical restoration and exploratory redesign of a vintage 9×12 reflex camera.

Project

Work includes diagnosing the mirror mechanism, investigating shutter cloth repair and considering a custom back compatible with contemporary large-format film holders.

The project documents inspection, measurement and design decisions without suggesting that the restoration is already complete.

Format9×12 cm
StatusWork in progress
MethodsMechanical analysis · 3D design
