Camera adapters and small parts

“A small, precise part can connect systems separated by decades.”

Practical camera parts, conversions and storage designs.

Project

Published works include a non-destructive Praktica B 50/2.4 to M42 conversion and camera battery cases. Other mechanical experiments include mounting rings and custom battery housings.

The scope is not limited to cameras: practical fabrication is a method of addressing specific problems.
