About

My work has moved between medical research, pharmaceutical development and science education. Today I focus on educational development and how people learn through exploration, making and interaction.

Alongside research I design instruments, adapt cameras and make photographs. Photography has also led to exhibitions, collaborative work and editorial projects, including F12 and Gruppo F.

Based in Stockholm, Sweden.

Aesthetically resilient, thematically interrelated material for memory and projection.
